The first, comprehensive example (a UAV inspection) is described in _A Comprehensive Route Exchange Example for a UxV_. Coordinates and identifiers are illustrative; the Turin and Como places are real towns, but the coordinates are approximate and the vehicles, stations and tickets are fictitious.

=== Conventions used in all examples

* The structure follows the Route Exchange Model: `overview`, `start`, `segment` (a Point at the end of its stretch), `waypoint`, `end`, with `featureType`, `length`, `duration_s` and `timestamp`.
* Each route lists the registries it uses in `actionRegistries`, and the Systems it addresses in `targets` (overview).
* A *segment action* applies to the stretch ending at the segment's Point and is issued when the vehicle leaves the previous waypoint. A *waypoint action* is issued on arrival.
* `sequence` is per target and follows route order. `execution.ordering = sequential`: each action starts when the previous one is complete.
* Waypoint properties `stopId`, `parcels`, `timeWindow` are informative extensions.
* Contingencies are route-level `{condition, action}` pairs, using registry actions.

==== What to notice

* The stop framing is explicit: `delivery.arrive-at-stop` first, `delivery.depart-stop` last, for both S1 and S2.
* S1 delivers on a winch line. The handover is one action (`delivery.drop-package-line`), proof is a separate `delivery.capture-proof`.
* S2 is a landing handover: `vehicle.land` (Common Registry) → verify (QR code) → `delivery.handover` (`drop-zone`) → `vehicle.takeoff`.
* A segment action issues `delivery.notify-recipient` (approaching) two minutes before arrival.
* Contingencies cover recipient-unavailable (return the parcel), a blocked landing site (report and ask for help) and loss of link.

* `execution.onFailure` is `continue`: a failed stop does not stop the round.

==== What to notice

* Four stops, a pickup (S3), and a mid-round charging stop. Time windows are waypoint properties.
* S2 is age-restricted: `delivery.verify-recipient` with `id-check` and `ageCheck: 18`, then a handover to the recipient with a signature.
* S4 is delivered to a safe place with photo proof.
* `delivery.park` frames the curb stay at every stop, with a five-minute limit.
* The contingencies schedule a reattempt for the next day and report a parking problem.
