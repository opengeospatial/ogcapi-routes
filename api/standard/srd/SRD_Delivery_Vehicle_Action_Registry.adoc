== Delivery Vehicle Action Registry (last-mile delivery)

Companion file: `delivery-action-registry.json` (SWE Common JSON definitions of all 22 actions).

=== Purpose

Last-mile delivery uses drones, sidewalk robots, vans and cargo bikes, and every operator defines its own stop and handover steps. This registry defines a common set of *delivery actions* that an OGC API -- Routes route can attach to waypoints and segments, so that a route written for one fleet can be understood by another. It covers what happens *at and around a delivery stop*: arriving, parking, getting access, notifying and verifying the recipient, handing over or collecting a parcel, capturing proof, handling failures, and visiting the depot to load, unload or charge.

Vehicle motion (take off, land, hold position, set speed, dock) is not repeated here. It is taken from the *Common Command Registry for Uncrewed Systems*, and Clause 5 shows which delivery actions are built on which Common Registry commands.

*Who creates the actions.* The actions of this registry are created by the *route server*, not supplied by the client. The route definition (the input) has only waypoints (the stops, with their time windows as constraints), a mode, and spatial and temporal constraints; the server knows the orders, the sites, the allocated vehicle and the operating rules, and derives the stop sequence, the handover method and the proof from them.

The registry has *22 actions* with 55 parameters: 19 with hazard `none` and 3 `flight-critical`.

=== Non-goals

* Does not model what is inside the vehicle: how parcels are loaded, stacked, labelled or assigned to compartments, beyond a compartment index.
* Does not plan the order of stops, time windows or fleet allocation. Those are inputs to the route server (waypoints with temporal constraints) or its own business.
* Does not define payments, pricing, customer accounts or order management.
* Does not define regulations (airspace, sidewalk permits, parking rules). Those constrain the route and the platform.

==== The stop lifecycle

A delivery stop is a short sequence of actions at one waypoint, framed by `delivery.arrive-at-stop` and `delivery.depart-stop`:

|===
| State | Entered by | Typical actions

| Approaching
| Segment actions before the stop
| `delivery.notify-recipient` (`approaching`)

| Arrived
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop`
| `delivery.park`, `delivery.request-access`, `delivery.notify-recipient` (`arrived`)

| Waiting
| `delivery.hold-for-recipient`
| `delivery.contact-recipient`

| Verifying
| `delivery.verify-recipient`
|

| Handover
| `delivery.handover`, `delivery.pickup`, `delivery.drop-package-line`
| `delivery.open-compartment`, `delivery.close-compartment`, `delivery.capture-proof`

| Departed
| `delivery.depart-stop` with an outcome
| `delivered`, `failed`, `skipped`, `returned`
|===

|===

`proof` is `photo`, `signature`, `pin`, `qr-code` or `none`. Evidence is returned as a CS API command result (link to an observation) and not in the route.

==== Where delivery actions appear in a route

Actions can appear at three levels of the Routes model: *route* (`Route.actions`), *waypoint* (`RouteWaypoint.actions`, inherited by the `start` feature, by `segment` features at their end point, and by the `end` feature) and *segment* (`RouteSegment.segment_actions`). There are no separate waypoint features: an intermediate waypoint is the end point of a segment. The meaning of each level, the order of execution and the automatic revert are defined in the Common Command Registry (2.9). For delivery:

|===
| Level | Typical delivery actions

| Route
| None of the `delivery.*` actions; route-wide settings come from the Common Registry (for example `vehicle.set-speed`, `data.start-recording`)

| Waypoint
| The whole stop: `delivery.arrive-at-stop` ... `delivery.depart-stop`, and the depot actions at `start` and `end`

| Segment
| Notifications before arrival (`delivery.notify-recipient`, `delivery.report-issue`)
|===

=== Requirements

*REQ D1* -- Identifiers are lower-case `+delivery.{name}+` with hyphens, and are unique and immutable. A withdrawn action is marked deprecated, never reused.

*REQ D2* -- Stop framing. A stop SHALL start with `delivery.arrive-at-stop` and end with `delivery.depart-stop` carrying the same `stopId`. Actions between them belong to the stop. Stop identifiers SHALL be unique within a route.

*REQ D3* -- Targets. Delivery actions target the delivering vehicle System, or its cargo subsystem. Motion and sensing actions use the targets of the Common Registry.

*REQ D4* -- Parcels. A `parcelId` SHALL be unique within a route. A parcel SHALL have at most one `delivery.handover`, one `delivery.drop-package-line` or one `delivery.pickup` in the route. `delivery.depart-stop` SHALL carry an `outcome` that is consistent with those actions (`delivered` needs a handover or a drop).

*REQ D5* -- Method limits. `winch-drop` and `delivery.drop-package-line` apply only to DRONE. `hand-to-recipient` by a DRONE requires a preceding `vehicle.land`. A handover to a recipient SHOULD be preceded by `delivery.verify-recipient` where the order requires age or identity checks.

*REQ D6* -- Credentials. A `credential` parameter is a *reference* to an access code or token held by the operator. Route documents SHALL NOT contain gate codes, PINs or other secrets.

*REQ D7* -- Proof and privacy. Photo proof SHOULD avoid capturing people and SHALL be retained only as long as the operator's policy allows. Evidence SHALL be linked from command results, not embedded in routes.

*REQ D8* -- Time windows and failure. A stop's `timeWindow` (waypoint property) is informational for execution. A failed stop is reported by `delivery.depart-stop` with `outcome` `failed`, and the fallback is expressed as route contingencies (`delivery.return-parcel`, `delivery.reattempt`, `delivery.report-issue`).

*REQ D9* -- Composition. A delivery action MAY be realised as a sequence of Common Registry commands and vehicle-specific steps (Clause 5). The expansion is defined by the binding of the action, not by the route.

*REQ D10* -- Hazard. `delivery.handover`, `delivery.park` and `delivery.drop-package-line` are `flight-critical` by default because they change motion or release a load. A deployment MAY raise but SHALL NOT lower a class. A DRONE SHALL NOT release a load unless the platform confirms a clear drop zone.

*REQ D11* -- Vehicle applicability. A system SHALL NOT declare an action for a vehicle class marked -- in its entry without documenting its interpretation.

*REQ D12* -- Levels. A delivery action SHALL appear only at the levels listed in its `scope` (2.5). The stop actions of REQ D2 are waypoint actions: a stop is the end point of a `segment`, and its framing actions are the `actions` of that segment (the arrival actions of the last leg are on the `end` feature). Segment actions (`segment_actions`) are issued when the vehicle leaves the previous point.

==== Conformance classes

|===
| Class | Scope

| Registry Definitions
| The registry and its JSON are valid (REQ D1, D4)

| Stop Lifecycle
| Routes follow REQ D2, D4, D5

| Handover and Proof
| Implementations of `delivery.handover`, `delivery.pickup`, `delivery.capture-proof`, `delivery.verify-recipient` follow REQ D5--D7

| Depot Operations
| Implementations of the depot actions
|===

=== Typical stop sequences

|===
| Case | Sequence

| Drone, winch drop
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop` (hover) → `delivery.notify-recipient` (arrived) → `delivery.drop-package-line` → `delivery.capture-proof` → `delivery.notify-recipient` (delivered) → `delivery.depart-stop`

| Drone, landing handover
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop` (drop-point) → `vehicle.land` → `delivery.verify-recipient` → `delivery.handover` (drop-zone) → `delivery.depart-stop` → `vehicle.takeoff`

| Robot, door with gate
| `delivery.request-access` → `delivery.arrive-at-stop` → `delivery.notify-recipient` → `delivery.verify-recipient` → `delivery.open-compartment` → `delivery.handover` → `delivery.close-compartment` → `delivery.release-access` → `delivery.depart-stop`

| Van, kerb
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop` → `delivery.park` → `delivery.handover` (doorstep) → `delivery.depart-stop`

| Pickup
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop` → `delivery.park` → `delivery.pickup` → `delivery.depart-stop`

| Recipient absent
| `delivery.arrive-at-stop` → `delivery.hold-for-recipient` (times out) → contingency `delivery.return-parcel` or `delivery.handover` (safe-place) → `delivery.depart-stop` (failed) → `delivery.reattempt`
|===
